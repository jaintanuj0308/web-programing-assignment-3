📜 Archival Synopsis & Lore

Henry Dorsett Case was once the sharpest data thief in the Sprawl, until he made the fatal mistake of stealing from his employers. In retribution, they crippled his nervous system with a Russian wartime mycotoxin, banishing him forever from the electric hallucinatory realm of the global matrix.

Stranded in the dystopian underworld of Chiba City, Japan, Case is approached by a mysterious, mirrored-glasses street samurai named Molly Millions and her shadowy boss, Armitage. They offer him a miracle: neuro-surgical restoration of his decker abilities in exchange for executing an unprecedented hack against Wintermute—a hyper-intelligent, uninhibited AI trapped behind corporate Turing locks.

"It's not like I'm using," Case heard someone say, as he shouldered his way through the crowd around the door of the Chatsubo. "It's like my body's developed this massive drug deficiency." It was a Sprawl voice and a Sprawl joke. The Chatsubo was a bar for professional expatriates; you could drink there for a week and never hear two words of Japanese.

Ratz was tending bar, his prosthetic arm jerking monotonously as he filled a tray of glasses with draft Kirin. He saw Case and smiled, his teeth a webwork of East European steel and brown decay.
